# Typo brand

Typo keeps Quire's brand, logo direction 1b from the Claude Design file "Quire Brand", with its own wordmark. A quire is a gathering of folded sheets. The mark is three pages in a stack: the one in front is the note you write, the others are the folder behind it.

- **Mark:** three 46 × 60 sheets with 6-unit corners in a 100-unit box, each offset 11 across and 10 down from the one behind. Front to back: violet `#7A4FBA`, `#C9BFAE`, `#E1DBCF`.
- **Wordmark:** "typo" in lowercase Instrument Sans Semibold, tracking −4.5% (−0.045 em), line height 1.
- **Lockup:** mark and wordmark in a row. The gap is a quarter of the mark's width. At 52 px type the mark is 48 px with a 14 px gap. The lockup works down to 16 px tall. Below that, use `mark-small.svg`.
- **App icon:** `icon-paper.svg` for light docks, `icon-ink.svg` for dark ones. Below 24 px use the `-16` files, which drop the back sheet.
- **On dark:** `mark-on-ink.svg` with violet `#B597E8` and wordmark colour `#E9E3D8`. On the accent colour: `mark-on-accent.svg` with a white wordmark. For print: `mark-one-colour.svg`.
